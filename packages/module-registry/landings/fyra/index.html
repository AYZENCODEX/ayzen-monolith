01 / the workspace

Money has
more than
one story.

Fyra brings a set of practical finance tools into AYZEN: a transaction ledger, business and personal books, obligations, assets, investments, and views that help connect the parts. Not a promise of magic. A place to keep the moving parts legible.

01 / RECORD≡

Ledger & activity

Track entries by type and status. Import transactions from CSV and export filtered records as CSV or PDF.

02 / POSITION⇅

What you own & owe

Dashboard summaries cover balance, net worth, receivables, payables, lending, borrowing, investments, expenses and upcoming dues.

03 / PLAN◔

Budgets & projections

Set category-based monthly limits and review trend-based scenarios, including receivables-collected and payables-paid cases.

04 / DOCUMENT▤

Invoices & evidence

Invoice flows include line items, tax and discounts, branding, payment agreements and attachments on entries.

05 / UNDERSTAND↗

Reports & insight

Explore profit and loss, cash-flow, balance-sheet, trial-balance and custom reports, plus analytics surfaces in the code.

06 / BUILD◎

Goals & accounting

Maintain accounts and journals, track financial goals, and use multi-book support for separate business and personal books.

02 / a more structured layer

From entries
to accounts.

Beyond a list of transactions, the source includes a double-entry accounting layer: a chart of accounts, journal entries and core financial statements. The intent is a clearer trail from the small line item to the shape of the whole.

04 / source review

What the finance
module includes.

A code-level review of the AYZENCODEX/ayzen-monolith finance frontend, API client, shared finance types and authenticated route. These are implementation findings, not a production certification.

Source files reviewed

Fyra is a real module in the app.

The route table mounts the finance dashboard at /finance and /finance/dashboard. The workspace sits behind AYZEN’s authenticated application shell.

✓

Ledger operations are wired

Authenticated entry list, create, update and delete, repayments, CSV import and CSV/PDF export, with typed filtering.

✓

Accounting reports are represented

Source includes chart of accounts, journals, trial balance, balance sheet, income statement, cash-flow reporting and asset depreciation endpoints.

✓

Planning and overview are present

Dashboard summary, budget tracking, net-worth tracking, goals, currencies, recurring entries and analytics surfaces.

✓

Invoice tooling is substantial

Invoice code covers line items, configurable tax and discount, branding, payment methods, agreements and invoice actions. Delivery and settlement were not verified.

✓

AI-assisted features exist in the client

API methods exist for quick-entry preview and commit, natural-language queries, anomaly checks and recurring suggestions. Their quality was not assessed.

Source-review boundary: this page describes what was visible in source files. No live account data, API calls, financial calculations, access-control tests or compliance controls were independently verified.
