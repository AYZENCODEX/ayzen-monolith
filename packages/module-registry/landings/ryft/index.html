        How P2P works

        Three steps, with funds protected until the trade is confirmed.

        1. Choose an offer

        Pick a merchant by price, limits and payment method, then enter your amount.

        2. Pay the seller

        Send the fiat amount with the listed method. The crypto is held in escrow meanwhile.

        3. Receive your crypto

        Once the seller confirms payment, the crypto is released to your Ryft wallet.
