Questions

Short answers about this preview.

Are these live listings?

No. Everything on this site is sample data. Listings, prices and P2P offers are placeholders until the backend is connected.

How are subscription prices set?

Each service has a monthly price. Longer plans take a percentage off, so the 12-month plan costs less per month than the 1-month plan.

How does P2P work?

Choose Buy or Sell, an asset, a currency and a payment method. Offers are sorted by best price. You can open an offer to see its limits, but trading is not connected yet.

Can I use dark mode?

Yes. Use the toggle in the header. The site follows your system setting until you choose one.
