      Governance

      Autonomy with guardrails.

      Zynth acts with the caller’s access and nothing more. Every tool call is checked against policy first, and the sensitive ones wait for an owner.

      Policy decisionsstreaming
        Permission tiers

        Three tiers, one rule: default deny.

        Read

        ai.execute

        Runs immediately for any member with access. Nothing is changed.

        Write

        ai.execute

        Runs inside the caller’s own scope, so changes stay limited to what that member could do directly.

        Approval

        ai.manage

        Held until an owner approves. Covers sending, sharing, inviting and moving money.

        What holds on every run.

        Roles

        Access comes only from explicit grants: members hold ai.execute, owners hold ai.execute and ai.manage.
