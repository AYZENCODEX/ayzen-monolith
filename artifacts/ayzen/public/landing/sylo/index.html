Three doors, tried in order

      
The Vault has its own lock. It never accepts your AYZEN account password or your login 2FA.

    

    

      

        1
Passkey
If you've registered one, it's the first and fastest way in.

      

        2
Vault PIN and 2FA
No passkey on hand? Use your Vault PIN or password plus the Vault's own authenticator code.

      

        3
Email code and PIN
If the second door isn't available, a code sent to your email and your Vault PIN get you through.

    

    

      
Entity details have a separate PIN. Even with the Vault open, viewing an entity asks for its Entity-view PIN.

      
Secrets are encrypted at rest. Vault secrets use AES-256-GCM, so what's stored isn't readable as plain text.

The careful parts

      
What you'll want when something goes wrong, or when you move on.

    

    

      

        
Activity log
Everything you've done inside the Vault: what happened, when and where.

        
Trash
Deleted entities can be restored within 30 days, then they're purged automatically.

        
Backup codes
Kept per entity, next to the account they belong to.

      

      

        
Encrypted snapshot
Export the whole Vault as one encrypted file.

        
Import and export
Bring entries in from another tool, or send them out as CSV or JSON.

        
Compliance report
Export your account and Vault activity for any date range as CSV or PDF.
