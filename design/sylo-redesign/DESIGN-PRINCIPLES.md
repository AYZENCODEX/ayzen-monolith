# Sylo design principles

1. **Mobile-comfortable first.** Sylo is used as an app on phones. Every screen must work in a single column at 390px.
2. **List, then a new page.** Wherever possible a list page only lists. Choosing a row opens a new individual page for that item (with a back link), instead of a split pane or a modal. Example: Access → Entity (list) → Aurelia Labs (its mailbox, 2FA codes and backup codes).
3. **One job per page.** Detail pages are stacked sections with a sticky jump bar, not side-by-side columns.
4. **Hierarchy in the sidebar** (Accounts, Access, Records, Other, Settings); on phones the sidebar becomes a bottom bar plus a menu.
5. **Section colours** come from the Sylo palette (brass, patina, copper, slate, moss); no gradients or glow.
