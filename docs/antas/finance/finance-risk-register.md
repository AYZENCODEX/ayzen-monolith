# Finance extraction: remaining-risk register

Status: FIN-WP-9, written 2026-10-20 against the WP-8 tree. Companion to `finance-final-validation.json` (BLOCKED) and `finance-evidence-index.md`.
Basis: static inspection plus the runs listed in `reports/antas/finance/wp9-logs/`. Nothing here claims a PASS.

The extraction is complete in code: 109 of 109 route handlers are adapters over `finance/application/*`, no `lib/finance-*` shim remains, and the boundary scan passes. What is **not** established is that the code compiles, passes its database tests, or behaves in staging at this revision. The register is ordered so the items that block a PASS come first.

Severity means: **High** = can lose or corrupt money or data, or blocks the PASS; **Medium** = wrong behavior, weak proof, or an operational step that can be missed; **Low** = hygiene.
